Install

PC: use the install icon in Chrome/Edge's address bar. iPad: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Both give you a full-screen app that works offline for your saved data.

Sync across devices

Keep notes, checkpoints, favorites, watch later, completed videos, history and resume positions the same on every device. It is encrypted on your device before it is uploaded to your own relay, so nobody else can read it. Stores it on your relay (Cloudflare) or in your own Google Sheet.

To add another device, use Show QR in the next card. It carries the sync key too.

Use Google Sheets instead: step by step
  1. If sync is on, tap Turn off sync first (you can keep or delete the cloud copy).
  2. Tap Copy the script below.
  3. Open sheets.new in a browser to create a blank Google Sheet.
  4. Click Extensions, then Apps Script.
  5. Select all the sample code, delete it, and paste the script. Click the save icon.
  6. Click Deploy, then New deployment. Click the gear icon and choose Web app.
  7. Set Execute as to Me and Who has access to Anyone. Click Deploy.
  8. Click Authorize access, choose your account. If Google says the app is unverified, click Advanced, then Go to project, then Allow. It is your own script.
  9. Copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec).
  10. Back here, tap Use Google Sheets instead and paste that URL.
  11. Tap Turn on sync. Then use Show QR on this device to set up your others.
Set up another device

Copy your relay and channels to another device with a QR code. If sync is on, the sync key comes along too, so keep the QR private. Without sync, use Export Backup for notes and history.

Network

In the Android app, requests go straight to YouTube. In the browser (PC/iPad) they must go through a relay. Free public relays are unreliable, so add your own (free Cloudflare Worker, see README) below, then tap Test relays.
